# Advisor evaluation lens

This reference incorporates useful evaluation patterns seen in Supervisor-Skills without copying its scoring text.

## Early fatal-flaw gate

Check before weighted scoring:

- the core mechanism is already refuted by the project's own evidence;
- the claimed novelty disappears against verified closest work;
- required data/hardware/access cannot be obtained inside the project window;
- the measurement cannot distinguish the mechanism from confounders;
- the simplest control would make the proposed complexity unnecessary;
- the contribution cannot be stated in one defensible sentence.

A critical flaw short-circuits the route. Do not decorate a rejection with optimistic aggregate scores.

## Contribution axes

Use only relevant axes: problem significance, mechanism clarity, effectiveness, efficiency, robustness, cost, breadth, reproducibility, and deployment value. Start from neutral evidence, not enthusiasm. Label mechanism-based promise separately from measured support.

## Capability/lifecycle fit

Estimate calendar time, effective researcher hours, implementation depth, data/hardware readiness, dependency risk, and target-venue expectations. A strong abstract idea may still be the wrong route for the current project.
